# Wholesale Starter Kit — Free

**The three tools every beginning real-estate wholesaler actually needs, free.**

Wholesaling, in plain words: you find a property owner who wants to sell,
you get the property under contract at a price that leaves room, and you
assign that contract to a cash buyer who closes on it. Your profit is the
assignment fee — the spread between your contract price and what the buyer
pays. No bank loan, no buying the house yourself.

The reason most beginners stall isn't hustle. It's math done on vibes and
seller calls with no structure. This kit fixes both.

## Who this is for

- You're new to wholesaling and your "offers" so far are guesses.
- You've talked to sellers but hang up without knowing if there's a deal.
- You want the working structure first, before you spend anything.

## The rule this kit runs on

**No-guess math.** Every offer shows the exact formula and the exact
numbers. If an input is missing — repair scope, payoff, HOA or lien
amounts, closing costs — you flag it as missing. You never paper over a
gap with a confident-sounding number. Sellers can smell fake math, and
fake math kills deals at the closing table anyway.
